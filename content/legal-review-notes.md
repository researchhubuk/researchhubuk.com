They are not a verification of the business's legal compliance or its offline operations.

Before relying on them as final business policies, confirm:

- Legal proprietor/entity name, business postal address, jurisdiction and any required registration/VAT details. Add the controller identity/address to the privacy notice and supplier identity/address to the terms.
- Hosting/CDN provider, server logs and retention, email/messaging account configuration, any support personnel/subprocessors, processing locations and applicable transfer safeguards. Confirm whether production infrastructure adds cookies or analytics absent from the repository.

Ensure express requests/acknowledgements are obtained where needed before early service performance; a website clause alone does not obtain consent.
