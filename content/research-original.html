Our Research Support Services in the UK

Research Proposal Support

The research proposal is a document in which the researcher identifies the areas to be investigated, its significance and methods through which the investigation can be conducted. Here, we provide information on formulating research questions, setting objectives and rationale, and structuring a proposal depending on university requirements.

Literature Review Assistance

Writing a literature review is not only about synthesizing existing pieces of writing. Students should be able to find suitable material, compare the results found, realize research gaps and discuss them. Our instructions are concentrated on literature search, themes, sources and structuring.

Dissertation Research Support

Many dissertations for undergraduate, master's and MBA degree holders may be expected to carry out in-depth research. We help our students with planning their research, chapter writing, writing literature reviews, methodology preparation and presenting research results.

Research Methodology Guidance

Selecting the right research methodology is very important for a good research plan. We guide students regarding different research methodologies like qualitative, quantitative, and mixed methodologies. Our guidance also covers research design, data collection methods, and ethics of research.

Research Paper and Academic Writing Support

In order to write research papers, there is a need for the provision of argument, evidence, and logic. With our help, students will be able to organize academic material and relate findings to the current literature.

Research Editing and Proofreading

It may be necessary to conduct a final check for a research paper prior to submission. The editing services that we provide include the checking of grammar, academic writing style, consistency, paragraph flow, referencing, and presentation, without changing the student’s meaning.

FAQs About Research Support Services

What do research support services mean?

Research support services offer academic assistance for planning, conducting, organising, and reviewing research studies. In case you require assistance, it can cover research proposal writing, literature review, methodology advice, research in dissertations and academic editing.

Could Research Hub UK be of any help with a research proposal?

Yes. We will be able to assist you with research questions, objectives, rationale, structure of the proposal, and selection of methodology as per your assignment instructions.

Can you help me with a literature review?

Yes. Literature review assistance can involve locating academic sources, assessing published studies, developing thematic analysis, finding research gaps, and improving critical analysis.

Do you offer any assistance for my dissertation work?

We give advice on different phases of undergraduate, Master's and MBA dissertation projects, which include research planning, literature review, methodology, and academic presentation.

Could you assist me in choosing a research method?

We can assist you in grasping the approaches such as qualitative, quantitative, and mixed methods and assessing their applicability to your research question. The choices that you make at the end need to align with your project needs and academic advice.

What fields can conduct research?

Research assistance could be considered in a variety of topics such as business, finance, legal studies, medicine, psychology, social science, computing & engineering, etc., as per the requirement.

What should I provide when asking for research assistance?

Tell us about your research topic, assignment description, academic level, number of words required, deadline, methodology requirements, and assessment criteria. Your existing drafts or university guidelines will also be helpful.

Can I ask for assistance with just one part of my research?

Yes. You have the option to ask for help in a particular part of your assignment, be it the research proposal, literature review, methodology, discussion of results, or conclusion.
