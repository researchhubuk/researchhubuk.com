Our Mission

Our mission is to provide clear, structured and accessible academic support for students across the UK. It is obvious that each coursework, assignment, research project and dissertation has its own set of specific requirements, and therefore, we take into account each individual task and its specific features, depending on the student’s area of study, academic level, the university and the assignment.

We strive to simplify academic tasks through targeted help in academic writing, research, evidence, referencing, structuring and presentation. As opposed to the universal way of helping students, we concentrate on individual requirements of each particular task.

FAQs About Research Hub UK

What is Research Hub UK?

Research Hub UK is an online research help service that offers assistance to the students studying at higher educational institutes in the United Kingdom with assignments and essays.

What academic services are provided by Research Hub UK?

We provide assignments, essays, coursework, dissertations (proposals and PhD theses), literature review, research proposal and paper, case studies, proofreading, BTEC, HNC, ATHE, resit assignments.

Which subjects does Research Hub UK support?

The topics that we cover include Business and Management, Accounting and Finance, Law, Nursing and Healthcare, Psychology, Social Science, Computing, Engineering, Education, Criminology, Pharmacy, and Architecture.

Does Research Hub UK support students from different UK universities?

Certainly. Our assistance is meant for those learners pursuing their academic studies in the United Kingdom’s various universities and colleges. We base our assistance on the assignment guidelines provided by the learners themselves.

Can you support Master's and PhD-level academic work?

Yes, our assistance covers master's dissertation help, MBA project help, research proposal, literature review, methodology, PhD dissertation help, academic editing and all advanced research needs.

Do you support BTEC, HNC, ATHE and other qualifications?

Certainly, yes. It will be possible for us to help you work on requirements that are related to qualifications and awarding bodies like Pearson BTEC, ATHE, OTHM, Qualifi, NCC Education, ABE, and CMI, among others.

What information is needed when reaching out to Research Hub UK?

Give the following details of your assignment; the project brief, assignment questions, academic level, word count, deadline, marking rubric, and other institutional requirements. Additional files may also be included where required.

How do I get started with Research Hub UK?

Simply submit your requirements and your assignment brief. We'll go through the information you have provided and tell you about the best academic assistance for your particular assignment.
