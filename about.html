WHO WE ARE

Support for every stage of your academic journey.

Research Hub UK provides academic writing and research support for students studying at UK universities and colleges. Our work covers assignments, essays, coursework, dissertations, research proposals, literature reviews and proofreading.

We start with your own brief, marking criteria and module guidance. This helps us shape our support around your subject, academic level, deadline and university requirements.

Our aim is to help you develop your understanding, organise your ideas and improve the clarity of your academic work. Use our guidance in line with your institution's academic integrity requirements.
