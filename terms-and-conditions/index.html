About Research Hub UK

Research Hub UK offers guidance and learning services for students enrolled in universities and colleges located in the UK. Our services are aimed at helping you become familiar with academic guidelines and develop your own research and writing skills.

We offer only guidance, feedback and learning assistance. We do not create any work for you to use as your own. The provision of the service and its scope will depend on your needs, your academic level and the information that you have provided. Our guidance includes the following:

  • Assignment brief understanding.
  • Feedback on the organization and argument in your essay and case study writing.
  • Help with writing dissertations for undergraduates, masters, and MBA students.
  • Guidance with writing a research proposal and literature review.
  • Research methods and academic writing advice.
  • Academic editing and proofreading of your own papers.
  • Advice with referencing and citing sources.
  • Research and study skills advice for students studying BTEC, HNC, HND, ATHE and other qualifications.
  • Advanced research advice including PhD.

Education Guidance from Research Hub UK is provided in order to assist students in meeting the academic requirements, gaining research skills, and enhancing their academic work.

Our services aid in learning and academic development. We do not undertake any assessed work for submission. It will always be up to the student to complete their own assessments and ensure that they adhere to their institution’s rules regarding academic integrity and submitting work that demonstrates their own efforts.

We provide our services with reasonable care and skill in accordance with the Consumer Rights Act 2015. Depending on the chosen service we offer, our assistance may include:

  • Critique of your academic arguments and critical analysis.
  • Advice on research methodology.
  • Assessing the structure, language and presentation of your academic writing.
  • Proofreading, editing and referencing assistance.
